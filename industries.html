Every industry has supply chain and procurement dynamics that generic frameworks miss. We work from the specifics — not from templates.

01
Manufacturing

Steel, aluminium, FMCG, chemicals — from China+1 transitions to multi-regional network design and CBAM-driven cost restructuring.

Manufacturers are operating in conditions their existing footprint was not designed for: demand volatility, supplier concentration risk that has shifted but not disappeared, and a carbon cost layer arriving via CBAM that most internal estimates have understated. We work on the structural decisions — network design, supplier ecosystem, carbon strategy — not dashboards.

Network Redesign

Multi-regional manufacturing footprint review balancing cost, risk, and market access — including China+1 transitions.

CBAM Compliance

Product-level carbon framing for EU-bound steel and aluminium — turning compliance into a sourcing decision input.

Production Planning

Demand-driven scheduling that accounts for supplier reliability, capacity constraints, and forecasting limits.

Supplier Ecosystem

Systematic qualification of alternative suppliers in new geographies — built before disruption forces it.

02
Commodities & Trading

Metal trading, agri-commodities, energy — price risk, trade corridor optimisation, and counterparty governance.

Commodity trading houses operate with information asymmetry as the edge. Real-time pricing inputs, geopolitical risk signals, freight cost volatility, and a CBAM cost layer that affects deal economics all need to be reasoned about together. We work on the intelligence infrastructure and the decisions it informs — not on reporting layers.

Price Risk Framing

Steel, aluminium, copper, crude, iron ore — price intelligence embedded in trade decisions, not parallel to them.

Trade Corridor Analysis

GCC–India–EU corridor mapping with freight cost, duty, and carbon cost framing for every realistic route option.

Chokepoint Risk Scoring

Red Sea, Hormuz, Malacca — disruption probability and impact framing for routing and insurance decisions.

Carbon Cost Modelling

CBAM cost per tonne per product — factored into trade P&L before execution, not after the contract is signed.

03
Logistics & Freight

Ocean, air, road freight — carrier strategy, route risk management, chokepoint monitoring, and contract governance.

Logistics is no longer a back-office function. War risk surcharges, port congestion, and regulatory changes have made freight a strategic line item. We work on carrier strategy, route risk, freight cost governance, and the contract terms that determine who carries risk when something goes wrong.

Carrier Strategy

Carrier selection and performance frameworks — moving beyond rate negotiation to total cost and reliability.

Route Risk Management

Pre-designed routing alternatives for critical corridors with cost and time impact understood in advance.

Freight Audit

Spend visibility and audit design — recovering value from the gap between contracted and billed amounts.

Contract Governance

Force majeure clauses, surcharge mechanisms, and liability allocation built for the current risk environment.

Procurement-Driven Businesses

Large buyers across sectors — strategic sourcing, supplier intelligence, category strategy, and contract governance.

Some businesses live or die on procurement: hospitality groups, healthcare systems, large retailers, infrastructure operators. We focus on structural improvements — spend visibility, category strategy, supplier governance, and the technology architecture that supports it. Not systems implementations. Decisions.

Spend Analysis & Category Strategy

Supplier Intelligence

Contract Governance

Contract structures that allocate risk appropriately — and the governance to enforce them when conditions change.

Source-to-Pay Architecture

Technology selection grounded in operational reality, not vendor pitch decks.

05
Infrastructure & Construction

Projects, MEP, capital goods — materials sourcing, subcontractor risk, and project procurement governance.

Project businesses face procurement and supply chain problems that are different in kind, not degree. Materials price volatility hits project cash flow directly. Subcontractor financial health is rarely systematically monitored. We work on the specific operational realities of project environments — not corporate procurement templates applied to project conditions.

Materials Sourcing Strategy

Sourcing strategy for project-critical materials — accounting for price volatility, lead time risk, and trade route exposure.

Subcontractor Risk

Financial health monitoring and performance management — before insolvency surfaces during a critical phase.

Project Procurement Process

Procurement process design aligned with project delivery realities, not corporate procurement templates.

Logistics Risk Assessment

Trade route and chokepoint analysis for the specific corridors serving your projects — contingency routing built in.
